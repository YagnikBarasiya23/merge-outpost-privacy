Merge Outpost: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Merge Outpost ("the game") on Android or iOS, who receives it, and what you can do about it. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Who we are

Merge Outpost is made by Yagnik Barasiya, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("the developer", "we"). Contact: yagnikbarasiya2306@gmail.com. The developer is responsible (the "controller") for the crash reports described below.

The age question

The first time the game starts, it asks for your year of birth. It turns the answer into one of three groups: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over. Only the group is kept, on your device. The year itself is not kept and is not sent anywhere. The question is asked again only if the game is reinstalled or its data is cleared.

Players under 13

For players under 13, the game requests and shows no adverts, does not start the advertising software, and sends no crash reports (any kept on the device are deleted). The game sends nothing to the developer or to Google. If you use a link on the Help card, it opens your browser or mail app, which follow their own privacy policies.

What is kept on your device

Your progress, best score, settings (sound, vibration, crash reports, tutorial) and age group are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer. On Android they are not included in device backups or transfers to a new phone. On iPhone and iPad they may be included in an iCloud or computer backup of the device, which Apple handles. Uninstalling the game removes them from the device.

Crash reports (players 13 and over)

While the "Crash reports" switch on the pause screen is on (it starts on), a report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics when the game crashes or hits an error. A report contains the technical details of the error, the device model, the operating system version, the game version, and random identifiers for this installation of the game (a Crashlytics installation ID and a Firebase installation ID). It does not contain your name, your advertising ID, your progress or anything you typed.

Reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days. Google processes them for the developer under the Firebase data processing terms; see the Firebase privacy and security page. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in finding and fixing faults. You can object at any time by turning the switch off, which also deletes any reports still waiting on the device.

Advertising (players 13 and over)

The game is free and shows adverts provided by Google AdMob: optional adverts you choose to watch for a reward, and adverts after some games end. Adverts are limited to content suitable for most audiences (rated PG at most).

To show and measure adverts, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derived;
  • information about how you interact with the game and with adverts;
  • diagnostic information, such as performance data.

Google uses this to deliver adverts, to limit how often you see an advert, to measure how adverts perform and to prevent fraud. Google does this as an independent controller, under its own policies: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive this information.

For players aged 13 to 17, every ad request is marked for teen treatment, so adverts are not personalised. For players 18 and over, adverts may be personalised depending on your choices. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, Google's consent message asks for your consent for personalised adverts and for storing or reading information on your device; that consent is the legal basis.

Your choices

  • "Privacy choices" on the pause screen (shown where the law requires it) lets you change your advertising consent at any time.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can allow or refuse tracking in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • The "Crash reports" switch on the pause screen turns crash reports off or on.
  • The game works fully without an internet connection. No adverts are shown and nothing is sent while you are offline; a crash report from that time waits on the device and is sent the next time you are online, only if crash reports are allowed.

Support emails

If you write to us, your email address and message are kept in the developer's email account (Gmail) so we can answer you, and are deleted within 12 months of the last message. "Contact support" in the game fills in the game version and operating system version; you can delete them before sending.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the game has no accounts, the developer cannot link a crash report to you without its installation ID; write to the contact address above and we will help as far as we can. For Google's advertising data, use Google's tools linked above. For players in India, the contact address above is also the contact for grievances.

International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on recognised safeguards for these transfers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Sale of personal information

The developer does not sell personal information. Advertising data is shared with Google as described above. In some US states this counts as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can limit it with the choices above.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
