Merge Outpost: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Merge Outpost ("the game") on Android or iOS, and what is done with it.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.

Your progress, best score and settings (sound, vibration, tutorial) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The game is free and shows adverts provided by Google AdMob. Some adverts are optional (you choose to watch one for a reward) and some appear after a game ends.

To show and measure adverts, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derived;
  • information about how you interact with the game and with adverts;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google uses this to deliver adverts, to limit how often you see an advert, to measure how adverts perform, and to detect fraud. Depending on your choices, adverts may be personalised. This information goes to Google, not to the developer. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before adverts are requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" on the pause screen.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks permission before tracking. You can change this in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • The game works fully without an internet connection. No adverts are shown and nothing is sent while you are offline.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not hold any personal information about players, and therefore does not sell or share any. The only third party that receives information is Google, as described under Advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
